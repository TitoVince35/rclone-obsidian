# Privacy policy: rclone-obsidian

_Last updated: 2026-11-26_

rclone-obsidian is a Google OAuth client used only by its owner, to let rclone sync files between their computer and their own Google Drive.

- **Who uses it:** only the owner of this repository. It is not offered to anyone else.
- **What data it accesses:** the files in the owner's Google Drive that rclone is configured to sync.
- **What is collected:** nothing. There is no server, no analytics and no third party. Files travel directly between the owner's computer and Google Drive.
- **Storage:** the OAuth token stays in the local rclone configuration on the owner's computer.
- **Sharing:** no data is shared with anyone.

Contact: open an issue on this repository.
